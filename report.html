How Each Theme Performs
Outlier exclusions stated where applied · paid status unverified · medians reduce distortion from unusually large posts
How themes are detected Community, Economy and Environment are scored against a fixed weighted-keyword dictionary. Site Activity uses a separate high-confidence rule: content must be dated June 1, 2026 or later and describe one of the validated Sampson Cay fieldwork or preparation activities; generic project, construction and location references do not qualify. Matching Facebook and Instagram captions count as one content item, while performance remains separate by platform. Closely matched content may carry more than one theme.
