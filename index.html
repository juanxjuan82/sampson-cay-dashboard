How Each Theme Performs
Organic results only where stated · median values reduce distortion from unusually large posts
How themes are detected Captions are scored against a fixed weighted-keyword dictionary. A theme is assigned when it scores at least four points and reaches at least 80% of the post’s strongest theme score; closely matched posts may carry more than one theme. Theme examples must use that theme as the post’s primary match and are ranked by theme strength weighted toward score purity before engagement. Posts with no qualifying match remain unclassified. AI is used only after classification to interpret the evidence.
